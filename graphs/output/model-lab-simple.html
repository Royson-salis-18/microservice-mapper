Model results, simply explained

We deliberately broke services, then checked whether the mapper identified the service that was actually broken. These two charts show the main trade-off.

Short answer: The live mapper named the broken service first most often. The learned model raised fewer false alarms, but it also named the right service less often. These results do not show that the learned model is a better overall choice.

1. How often did it name the right service first?

Each bar is the share of all 34 injected faults where the method both noticed the fault and put the actual broken service first. Higher is better.
