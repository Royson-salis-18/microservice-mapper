A failing microservice drags its callers down, and within a minute operators face dozens of alarms with no sign of which service started it. This project builds an unsupervised diagnosis platform that watches Docker containers from outside, over SSH, and needs no instrumentation, labels or GPU. It recovers the call graph from socket tables, models normal behaviour while removing the workload shared by all services, ranks the root cause with a random walk, and learns online how failures propagate. A hybrid of this model and robust rules alarms on container facts and statistical deviation. Evaluated on 62 blind faults in two real systems, 84 live faults and 178 synthetic faults, the hybrid names the true cause first for 71% of real faults against 63% for rules alone (not statistically significant), keeps false alarms on workload changes at 0%, and costs 0.37 ms per service.
