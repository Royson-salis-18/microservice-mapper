#Notations
S, N – the set of services and its size
xₛ(t) – the reading of service s at time t (eight metrics)
Δ – the collection period, 5 seconds
w(e), c(e) – weight and evidence confidence of edge e
level – 0 typical, 1 on the service's alarm line
L(t) – load factor (the median shift of the traffic-carrying signals)
λₛ, ρₛ – model level and rule score of service s
πₛ – random-walk share of service s
α – restart probability of the walk (0.15)
p̂(c ← x) – learned probability that trouble in x reaches its caller c
P(c) – risk that service c is reached from a suspected root
